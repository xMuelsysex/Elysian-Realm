# Honkai Impact 3rd Elysian Realm Lore Source Notes

## Purpose

Collect verifiable, fan-research-oriented sources for Honkai Impact 3rd's **Elysian Realm / 往世乐土** and **Thirteen Flame-Chasers / 逐火十三英桀**. This note is an index and interpretation aid for future persona/world configuration work; it must not copy official dialogue dumps, official story text, art, audio, or proprietary assets into the project.

## Provenance and Use Policy

- Use sources for **summary, relationship constraints, terminology, and research direction** only.
- Do not store official dialogue, full transcripts, official images, extracted game assets, or guide images in this repository.
- Treat generated daily-life behavior as original fan simulation content, not official canon text.
- Mark all future persona facts by provenance: `official-summary`, `wiki-summary`, `fan-analysis`, `project-authored`, or `generated`.
- HoYoverse official pages may be JavaScript-rendered; record their URLs and corroborate with accessible pages where browser extraction only returns `Loading...`.

## Consolidated Lore Anchors for Simulation Design

### Elysian Realm / 往世乐土

- Elysian Realm is presented as a memory/data space built from the memories of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers.
- It is associated with the Golden Courtyard and later World Serpent access; Eden, Mobius, Vill-V, Klein, and Aponia have important infrastructure/ownership/admin/fusion links.
- In-game Elysian Realm story is fragmented across main chapters, recollections, and events rather than a single strict order. Bilibili full-story index explicitly warns that recollections/events are not strictly chronological.
- The initial Elysian Realm mode story has three named chapters:
  1. 在无限的暗影之中
  2. 致世界上的另一个我
  3. 愿时光永驻此刻，愿明日——
- Later continuation happens in mainline chapters 29-31, especially the Everlasting Elysium / 致爱莉希雅 arc.
- Design implication: the project should model memories, fragments, events, and replayable perspectives instead of one linear chat log.

### Reusable Persona Constraints

For future persona JSON/YAML, store **summaries like these**, not official lines:

- Elysia: warm, playful, sincerely interested in people; acts as a social connector; should invite others into scenes but avoid becoming omniscient.
- Kevin: terse, mission-burdened, emotionally restrained; slice-of-life scenes can show awkward everyday attempts rather than only combat/sacrifice.
- Aponia: gentle but dangerous when care becomes control; should speak in calm, reflective constraints; avoid using her power as a universal mind-control shortcut.
- Eden: generous, artistic, nostalgic for the old era; useful for music, gatherings, gifts, and bittersweet reflection scenes.
- Mobius: intellectually ruthless and curious; should test boundaries, bargain, and provoke, but not silently override the world state.
- Pardofelis: practical, lucky, evasive, shopkeeper/trader; great for daily routines, market events, low-stakes schemes, and reluctant courage.
- Griseo/Kosma pair: color/art and protective reserve; useful for quiet observation scenes and nonverbal emotional cues.
- Sakura/Kalpas/Aponia/Pardofelis Dusk Street links: useful as relationship graph seeds, but tragedy should be summarized carefully.

## Project Design Implications

1. **Memory-first simulation is canon-compatible.** Elysian Realm itself is a memory/data space, so the planned memory stream, replay, and perspective-specific summaries fit the source material.
2. **Do not linearize everything.** The source story is fragmentary; future UI should support fragments, recollections, event provenance, and debug replay.
3. **Relationship graph matters.** Official relationship-map pages and wiki summaries show the Flame-Chasers are not interchangeable agents; every relationship should be directed and source-labeled.
4. **Daily-life MVP can lean on Golden Courtyard tone.** The special animation provides slice-of-life ensemble inspiration, but do not copy scenes or dialogue.
5. **Canon alignment must be behavioral, not textual.** Persona files should record temperament, values, relationship constraints, and boundaries as project-authored summaries.
6. **Gameplay GitHub repos are useful for names/aliases only.** They are not lore authorities and are often asset-heavy.
